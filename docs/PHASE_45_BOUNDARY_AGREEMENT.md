# PHASE 45 BOUNDARY AGREEMENT (v0.1 — DRAFT, not locked)

_Status: DRAFT until committed to docs/ on main. Level 2 document under
the AI Company Constitution v1.1. More restrictive than, and consistent
with, the Phase 44 agreements. The filesystem is the source of truth._

## Purpose

Determine, ON PAPER and WITHOUT RISKING CAPITAL, whether EmaCross 9/21
signals on R_100 with the existing 1%/2% bracket have positive
expectancy after costs.

This phase MEASURES. It does not improve, tune, or optimize. EmaCross
stays 9/21 and the bracket stays 1%/2% for the entire phase.

## Established facts (verified 2026-07-24, before this agreement)

- Bracket is fixed: stop 1.0% from entry, target 2.0% from entry
  (verified in committed signals). Risk:reward 1:2.
- Spread on R_100 is 0.2 points, observed constant across three samples
  at prices 562.4 / 562.45 / 563.3; `quote` sits at the midpoint of
  bid/ask. Round-trip cost therefore 0.2 points ~= 0.0356%.
- Net outcomes after cost: win +1.964%, loss -1.036%.
- **Break-even win rate = 34.5%.**
- `ticks_history` returns `quote` only (no bid/ask). Historical spread
  is therefore a STATED ASSUMPTION sampled from the live stream, not
  reconstructed from history.
- R_100 is a synthetic index generated by Deriv's random number
  generator, not a market. A trend-following rule has no structural
  reason to find an edge in a generated random walk. This phase tests
  that empirically rather than assuming either way.
- Available contract types on R_100 are options-style (multipliers,
  turbos, digits, CALL/PUT). There is no plain CFD. Mapping signals to
  an executable instrument is OUT OF SCOPE here and is a prerequisite
  for any future execution phase.

## Falsification criteria (SET BEFORE ANY DATA EXISTS)

The strategy is judged to have an edge ONLY if BOTH hold:

1. At least **1,000 resolved signals** in the evaluation sample.
2. Observed win rate is **>= 38%** under the primary position policy,
   i.e. clearly above the 34.5% break-even rather than within noise of
   it (at n=1,000, sampling variation alone moves win rate by roughly
   +/- 3 percentage points).

If the result is below that bar, the finding is: **EmaCross 9/21 on
R_100 does not have a demonstrable edge.** The response to that finding
is to accept it and stop.

EXPLICITLY FORBIDDEN as a response to a negative result:
- Changing the EMA periods and re-running.
- Changing the bracket ratio and re-running.
- Switching symbol (R_75, 1HZ100V, etc.) and re-running.
- Filtering signals by score, regime, or time of day post hoc.
Each of these is curve-fitting to noise. Any of them requires a new
phase, a new agreement, and explicit CEO approval recorded here.

## Position policies (both measured)

- **PRIMARY — one position at a time.** Enter on a signal; ignore all
  further signals until the bracket resolves. This is what could
  actually be executed and is the number that decides the phase.
- **DIAGNOSTIC — every signal independently.** Overlapping positions
  permitted; purely theoretical. Reported alongside, never used as the
  pass criterion.

Interpretation rule agreed in advance: if DIAGNOSTIC shows no edge, the
signal itself is dead regardless of execution. If DIAGNOSTIC shows edge
but PRIMARY does not, the finding is a capacity/execution problem, not
a signal problem — and it still does not pass this phase.

## Work items

**W45.0 — history fetch tool.** New file `tools/fetch_history.py`.
Read-only. Uses the existing `DerivRestOtpTransport` (which already
sends `ticks_history`) to page backwards and write ticks to disk.
Nothing under `engine/` is touched. CP3 is unaffected (its rule binds
imports under `engine/` only).

**W45.1 — offline signal generation.** Replay the stored tick series
through the REAL `EmaCross(9/21)`, `SimpleRegime`, and
`DescriptiveBracket` classes — imported, never reimplemented — to
produce signals identical in form to the live journals.

**W45.2 — resolution.** For each signal, walk the tick series forward
from its timestamp. Long: target = entry * 1.02, stop = entry * 0.99.
Short: target = entry * 0.98, stop = entry * 1.01. Record which is
touched first, or UNRESOLVED if the data ends first. Unresolved signals
are excluded from the win rate and reported separately.

**W45.3 — expectancy.** Apply costs and both position policies.
Report: resolved count, unresolved count, win rate, expectancy per
signal (%), and total return, for each policy.

## What this phase cannot conclude

A positive result would mean the signals showed an edge on a sample of
generated price data under stated cost assumptions. It would NOT
establish that the strategy is profitable in practice, that the result
persists out of sample, or that execution would achieve the modelled
fills. Those are separate questions for separate phases.
