## The question this phase asked

Do EmaCross 9/21 signals on R_100, with the fixed 1% stop / 2% target
bracket, have positive expectancy after costs — measured on paper, with
no capital at risk?

## Answer

No. Both position policies produce negative expectancy. The observed win
rate sits below break-even, and the entire naive confidence interval sits
below break-even.

| Measure | Primary (decisive) | Secondary (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Trades | 25,428 | 143 |
| Wins / Losses | 8,081 / 17,347 | 43 / 100 |
| Win rate | **31.78%** | 30.07% |
| Naive 95% CI | 31.21% – 32.35% | 22.55% – 37.59% |
| Block-based 95% CI | 24.19% – 34.46% | 13.49% – 35.85% |
| Expectancy per trade | **−0.081%** | −0.133% |
| Total return | −2,067.7% | −18.98% |

Reference points:
- **33.33%** — what a driftless random walk produces with barriers at
  −1% and +2%, by construction.
- **34.50%** — break-even after the measured 0.2-point spread.
- **38.00%** — the pass bar, locked 2026-07-24 before any data existed.

## Why the result is what it is

R_100 is a synthetic index generated by Deriv's random number generator.
It is not a market: there is no order flow, no supply and demand, no news.
A trend-following rule extracts value from persistence — the tendency of
moves to continue. A generated random walk has no persistence to find, so
the expected value of any such rule is approximately zero before costs and
negative after them.

The measured 31.78% is close to the 33.33% a driftless random walk
predicts. The strategy found approximately the amount of signal that was
available to find: none. Costs then push the result further negative.

This was flagged as a structural prior at the start of the phase and
deliberately tested rather than assumed. The phase exists because "it
probably won't work" is not evidence.

## What this phase does NOT conclude

- It does not conclude that EMA crossovers fail on real markets with
  genuine order flow. It tested one strategy on one synthetic instrument.
- It does not conclude that the engine is faulty. Every gate passed
  throughout; the measurement apparatus worked correctly and produced a
  trustworthy negative.
- It does not conclude that different parameters would fail. It also does
  not license testing them — see below.

## Forbidden responses (from the locked agreement, restated)

The following are NOT permitted as responses to this result:
- Changing EMA periods and re-running.
- Changing the bracket ratio and re-running.
- Switching symbol (R_75, 1HZ100V, etc.) and re-running.
- Filtering signals post hoc by score, regime, or time of day.

Each is curve-fitting to noise. On a randomly generated series, searching
parameter space until something looks profitable will eventually succeed
by chance and will not survive contact with new data. Any future work in
this direction requires a new phase, a new agreement, and explicit CEO
approval recorded in writing.
